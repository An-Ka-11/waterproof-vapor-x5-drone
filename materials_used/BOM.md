Bill of Materials - Stock Vapor X5 Waterproofing Project

Base Hardware:
Drone: Vapor X5 6S Analog Version FPV Drone

Preparation and Waterproofing Materials:
Isopropyl Alcohol: Used for cleaning PCBs from flux and other grit.
Silicone Conformal Coating: For general waterproofing of all boards.
Liquid Electrical Tape: On high power joints to provide even more protection.

Tools Used:
Hex screwdrivers
Fine tip brush
Angled tweezers
Soft-bristled toothbrush
